# Accessibility Specialist — Learning Path

> An engineer who ensures interfaces are inclusive, standards-compliant, and usable by everyone.

**Level:** Advanced · **Required:** 71 articles (~13.3 h) · **Optional:** 10 articles (~1.9 h)

Difficulty of required articles: Foundational 22 · Intermediate 49.

Follow the sections in order. Articles link into [`docs/`](../docs/); each shows its difficulty and estimated reading time. Full prerequisites for any article are in its domain's `graph.json` (see [GRAPH.md](../GRAPH.md)).

## Milestones

1. Apply WCAG and the ARIA model correctly
2. Engineer keyboard, focus, and screen-reader experiences
3. Build accessible widgets, forms, and live regions
4. Respect motion, contrast, and perceivable-UI requirements
5. Audit accessibility with automated and AT-based testing

## Expected skills

By completing the required articles you should be able to:

- WCAG/ARIA conformance in real components
- Keyboard, focus, and assistive-tech engineering
- Accessible widget and form patterns
- Automated and manual accessibility auditing
