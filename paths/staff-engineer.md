# Staff Engineer — Learning Path

> A technical leader who sets direction, drives consistency, and multiplies teams across a large frontend.

**Level:** Staff · **Required:** 95 articles (~26.4 h) · **Optional:** 17 articles (~3.3 h)

Difficulty of required articles: Intermediate 23 · Advanced 52 · Staff 20.

Follow the sections in order. Articles link into [`docs/`](../docs/); each shows its difficulty and estimated reading time. Full prerequisites for any article are in its domain's `graph.json` (see [GRAPH.md](../GRAPH.md)).

## Milestones

1. Drive technical strategy, RFCs, and cross-team standards
2. Architect composition and design systems at scale
3. Own release safety, incident response, and supply-chain risk
4. Multiply teams through DX, guardrails, and mentorship
5. Balance innovation against stability in technology choices

## Expected skills

By completing the required articles you should be able to:

- Technical direction and decision-making at scale
- Org-wide architecture, standards, and consistency
- Production reliability, security, and risk ownership
- Leadership, mentorship, and technology strategy
