# Senior Frontend Engineer — Learning Path

> A broad, autonomous engineer who owns features end-to-end with quality across the whole stack.

**Level:** Advanced · **Required:** 221 articles (~47.5 h) · **Optional:** 55 articles (~14.1 h)

Difficulty of required articles: Foundational 30 · Intermediate 152 · Advanced 39.

Follow the sections in order. Articles link into [`docs/`](../docs/); each shows its difficulty and estimated reading time. Full prerequisites for any article are in its domain's `graph.json` (see [GRAPH.md](../GRAPH.md)).

## Milestones

1. Own a feature end-to-end: UI, state, data, tests, a11y
2. Make deliberate rendering and state-architecture choices
3. Meet performance, security, and accessibility bars by default
4. Ship safely with CI, monitoring, and error tracking
5. Raise the bar through reviews and refactoring

## Expected skills

By completing the required articles you should be able to:

- Full-stack-of-frontend delivery with quality built in
- Sound state, data, and rendering architecture
- Performance, security, and accessibility as defaults
- Production operability and code stewardship
